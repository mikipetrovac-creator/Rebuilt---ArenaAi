Journey to the Sunken City and the Church of Saint Nicholas

The ancient Lycian coast stretches along Turkey's stunning Mediterranean shoreline, where turquoise waters conceal the remnants of civilisations lost to time. From the mysterious Sunken City of Kekova and the remarkable rock-cut tombs of Myra to the sacred Church of Saint Nicholas, this region is home to some of Turkey's most fascinating cultural and historical treasures.

The Church of Saint Nicholas in Demre attracts visitors from around the world who come to discover the life of the beloved bishop whose generosity inspired the legend of Santa Claus. Together with the ancient Lycian city of Myra and the sunken ruins scattered beneath Kekova's crystal-clear waters, it forms one of the most unique historical routes in the Mediterranean.

Beyond its breathtaking coastal scenery lies a rich heritage shaped by the Lycian civilisation, one of the most influential cultures of the ancient world. Impressive rock-cut tombs carved high into the cliffs, remarkably preserved Roman structures, and centuries-old churches offer a fascinating glimpse into the region's remarkable past.

Today, Demre, Myra, and Kekova remain among Turkey's most captivating cultural destinations. Our carefully crafted full-day tour from Alanya and Side takes you through spectacular mountain landscapes and scenic coastal roads to these extraordinary landmarks. Whether you're cruising above the Sunken City, exploring ancient ruins, visiting the Church of Saint Nicholas, or swimming in the turquoise waters of Kekova Bay, this unforgettable journey combines history, culture, nature, and adventure in a truly unique Mediterranean experience. Frequently asked questions

How do I book, and when will I receive confirmation?

Simply fill in the booking form on this page with your name, phone number, hotel, and preferred date. We will contact you via WhatsApp within 2 hours to confirm your reservation. No upfront online payment is required — payment is made on the day of the tour.

Where and when will I be picked up?

We offer free hotel pickup from Alanya and Side (and nearby hotels). Pickup times are typically between 07:00–08:00, depending on your hotel's location. Your exact pickup time will be confirmed via WhatsApp the evening before the tour.

What should I bring on the tour?

Please bring your passport or ID card (required for entry to the sites), comfortable walking shoes, swimwear and a towel for the boat swim stop, sunscreen, a hat, and a bottle of water. A camera or smartphone is highly recommended — you will want to capture the scenery!

Are children and infants welcome on the tour?

Yes, absolutely. Children aged 4–7 receive a discounted price. Infants aged 0–3 travel completely free of charge. Please note that free infant places do not include a separate seat on the bus — the child must be held on a parent's or guardian's lap throughout the journey.

What exactly will I see on the Demre, Myra and Kekova tour?

Three highlights in one day: a boat cruise over the Sunken City of Kekova (ancient ruins visible underwater), the rock-cut Lycian tombs and Roman theatre at Myra, and the Church of St. Nicholas in Demre — the real Santa Claus, who lived and was buried here in ancient Lycia.

How long is the day, and is it suitable for children and older travellers?

It's a full-day tour with an early start and a long but scenic coastal drive. There's a relaxing boat cruise in the middle, so it balances well. It's fine for children and older guests who are comfortable with a full day out — just bring sun protection, water, and comfortable shoes.

Is the St. Nicholas Church in Demre where Santa Claus is buried?

Yes. St. Nicholas — the historical figure behind Santa Claus — was a bishop in ancient Myra (today's Demre) in the 4th century, and the church built over his tomb still stands here. You visit it on the tour; it's one of the most important early Christian sites in Turkey.

What are the rock tombs at Myra?

The Lycian rock tombs at Myra are elaborate tombs carved directly into a cliff face over 2,000 years ago, some shaped like temple facades. Right beside them is a well-preserved Roman amphitheatre. Together they're one of the most striking ancient sites on Turkey's south coast, and a highlight of the tour.
