Discover Green Canyon — Turkey's Emerald Lake

High in the Taurus Mountains above Manavgat lies Green Canyon — a vast emerald-green reservoir framed by sheer limestone cliffs and pine forests. Fed by dozens of cold mountain springs, its water glows in a shade of green so intense that locals call the area Turkey's 'jade kingdom'.

The lake was created by the mighty Oymapınar Dam on the Manavgat River — a great arch dam about 185 metres high, one of the tallest in Turkey. Behind it the water reaches depths of around 100 metres, staying refreshingly cool and remarkably clear even at the height of summer.

Green Canyon is one of the very few canyons in Turkey you can explore by boat. From the pier, comfortable tour boats cruise across the lake and deep into the Big Canyon, where cliffs rise straight out of the water. Along the way there's a swimming break in the emerald water — an experience you won't forget.

Our relaxed day tour takes you from Alanya and Side through the foothills of the Taurus Mountains to the pier, includes the full boat cruise and lunch at the Green Lake restaurant, and gets you back to your hotel the same afternoon — nature, fresh mountain air and emerald water in one easy day. Frequently asked questions

How do I book, and when will I receive confirmation?

Simply fill in the booking form on this page with your name, phone number, hotel and preferred date. We'll contact you via WhatsApp, Viber, Telegram or MAX within 2 hours to confirm. No upfront online payment is required — you pay on the day of the tour.

Where and when will I be picked up?

We offer free hotel pickup from Alanya and Side (and nearby resorts). Green Canyon is much closer than most day trips, so pickup is at a comfortable morning hour — we board the boat between 10:00 and 10:30. Your exact time is confirmed via WhatsApp, Viber, Telegram or MAX the evening before.

What should I bring on the tour?

Bring your swimsuit and a towel if you'd like to swim during the break or at Green Lake, plus sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses and water. The spring-fed mountain water stays refreshingly cool even in summer. A camera or smartphone is a must — the scenery is unforgettable!

Are children and infants welcome on the tour?

Yes, absolutely. Children aged 4–7 receive a discounted price. Infants aged 0–3 travel completely free of charge. Please note that free infant places do not include a separate seat on the bus — the child must be held on a parent's or guardian's lap throughout the journey.

What is Green Canyon and why is the water so green?

Green Canyon is a stunning reservoir on the Oymapınar Dam near Alanya, surrounded by pine-covered mountains. The deep emerald-green colour comes from the mineral-rich water and the reflection of the surrounding forest. You explore it on a relaxed boat cruise with swimming stops in the calm, cool water.

Is Green Canyon suitable for a relaxed family day out?

Yes — it's one of the calmest, most relaxing tours from Alanya. The boat is stable, the water is calm, and there's time to swim, take photos, and enjoy lunch on board. It suits families, couples, and anyone who wants beautiful scenery without a strenuous day.
