Private boat

4 hoursEvery day · 10:00–14:00 or 16:00–20:00
No prepayment · pay on the day of the tour

Rent the whole boat just for your group: family, friends or a celebration. The boat sails every day from the marina in Alanya, in a morning or afternoon slot of 4 hours each.

Hotel transfer to the marina and back, food and soft drinks are included in the price. You are also welcome to bring your own food and drinks. The boat takes up to 12 people: a covered deck with sunbeds, a dining table and a cabin.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the private boat cost?

€350 for the whole boat for up to 6 people. Each additional person costs €20, up to a maximum of 12 people. So 12 people pay €470 in total.

What is included?

Hotel transfer to the marina in Alanya and back, food on board and soft drinks. You are also welcome to bring your own food and drinks.

When does the boat sail?

Every day, in two 4-hour slots: 10:00–14:00 or 16:00–20:00. Choose the slot in the booking form.

Do I have to pay in advance?

No. Send a booking request, we confirm within 2 hours, and you pay on the day of the trip.

Have a question?

Write to us and we will reply within 2 hours.
